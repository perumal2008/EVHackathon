You are a signal runner trapped inside a broken simulation. The world has two states: NOW and ECHO. Every shift changes the map. Reach the exit while collecting signal shards — but obstacles can move when reality changes.

No downloads. No external assets. Works offline in a modern browser.

Mission

  • Move with WASD or arrow keys.
  • Press Space to SHIFT between NOW and ECHO.
  • Collect every cyan shard, then reach the portal.
  • Red nodes are hazards. Touching one costs energy.
  • Each level changes the rules slightly.
